# AutoMix tuning

AutoMix tuning lets you set AutoMix's limits yourself, in place of what its Smooth and DJ styles set. You
choose how long a mix can run, how far it may change a song's tempo, and which parts of a mix it uses.

## Settings

- **Longest transition** is the longest a mix can run, up to 40 s. At 0, the style's own length applies:
  12 s for Smooth and 40 s for DJ.
- **Most tempo change** is the most AutoMix changes a song's tempo to match the other song, from **None**
  to **12 %**. **The style's** keeps 6 % for Smooth and 10 % for DJ.
- **Beat matching**, **Bass swap**, **Filters** and **Keep the pitch** start on. Turn one off to leave it
  out of every mix.

## Good to know

- The values apply only while AutoMix is on, with either style.
- Nothing here changes your saved settings. Turn the plugin off, and AutoMix uses its style's own values
  again.
