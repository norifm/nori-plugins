# AutoMix tuning

AutoMix's Smooth and DJ styles each set how long a transition may be, how far a song's tempo is moved to
match the other's, and which parts of a mix they use. This plugin sets them yourself.

## Settings

- **Longest transition**: up to 40 s; 0 keeps the style's (12 s Smooth, 40 s DJ).
- **Most tempo change**: none to 12 %, or the style's (6 % Smooth, 10 % DJ).
- **Beat matching**, **Bass swap**, **Filters**, **Keep the pitch**: turn one off to leave it out of
  every mix.

Nothing here changes your saved settings: turn the plugin off and the style's own values are back.
