# AutoMix tuning

AutoMix tuning sets AutoMix's limits in place of what its Smooth and DJ styles set. You choose how long a
mix can run, how far it may change a song's tempo, and which parts of a mix it uses. It applies only
while AutoMix is on.

- At 0, **Longest transition** keeps the style's own length: 12 s for Smooth and 40 s for DJ.
- **The style's** tempo change is 6 % for Smooth and 10 % for DJ.

Your saved settings stay as they are. Turn the plugin off, and AutoMix uses its style's values again.
