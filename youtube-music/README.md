# YouTube Music

YouTube Music adds YouTube Music profiles to nori, so you can search YouTube Music, play from it and
download its songs. Sign in to see your own Home, your library and your likes.

To add one, install the plugin and turn it on. Then, in **Settings › Profiles**, tap **Add profile** and
choose YouTube Music. Without signing in, you can search, play, download and see YouTube Music's Home.

## Sign in

- **Sign in to YouTube Music** opens Google's sign-in page in nori.
- **Paste cookies instead** takes the cookies of a signed-in browser session, as a Cookie header or the
  text of a cookies.txt file.

If your Google account has more than one channel, choose one in the profile's **Channel**.

Signed in, your library is your YouTube Music library. It has your saved albums, the artists you
subscribe to, your library's songs and your playlists. Favorites holds your liked songs. A favorite song
is a like on YouTube Music. Favorite albums and artists stay on this device. Playlists you make in nori
are private on YouTube Music.

## What it sends

nori sends your searches and what you open and play to YouTube. Signed in, every request carries your
session's cookies, which nori keeps on this device and renews when YouTube asks. Each song you play goes
into your YouTube history, which YouTube's recommendations learn from. If YouTube ends the session, nori
asks you to sign in again.

## Good to know

- This is best effort: YouTube can stop it working at any time.
- YouTube has one file for each song, so nori's quality settings do not apply to it. nori fetches the
  next song ahead, in full.
- Live videos are left out.
- Albums, Artists and Songs show the first page of each.
- Signing in through a plugin may be against YouTube Music's terms. Music still plays without signing in.
