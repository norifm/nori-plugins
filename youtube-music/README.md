# YouTube Music

Install it, turn it on, then add a YouTube Music profile. Signed in, Library is your YouTube Music
library: your saved albums, the artists you subscribe to, your library's songs, your playlists, and your
liked songs in Favorites.

## Good to know

- This is best effort: YouTube can stop it working at any time.
- Live videos are left out.
- Artists and Songs show the first page of each.
