# Showcase

Showcase shows each place and kind of row a plugin can fill, for people who write plugins. It changes
nothing in nori.

With it on, you see these:

- Its page holds one row of each kind: a switch, a choice, a level, segments, flags and a meter. Text
  and secret fields, an action, a status, a link, a note and a page of its own follow.
- A song's menu has **Show song facts**, and the menu of an album or a playlist has **Count tracks**.
- A song's **Details** has lines of its own.
- The player has a line of its own under the output row.
- The **Playing on** sheet shows its rows.

A change to a row, an action and the menu entries each show a notice.

## What it sends

Showcase has no network access. Its **Search the web** line in **Details** opens DuckDuckGo with the
song's artist and title, only when you tap it.
