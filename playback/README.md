# Speed and pitch

Speed and pitch sets how fast songs play and how high they sound, apart from each other. It can also
skip silence in songs, and make previous always go back a song. nori has no settings of its own for
these.

With **Previous always goes back a song** off, previous restarts the song once more than 3 seconds of it
played.

With **Skip silence** on, or a speed or pitch other than 1×, nori cannot use offload. The CPU decodes the
music, which uses more battery.
