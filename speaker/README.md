# nori speaker

nori speaker plays your music on a speaker that runs nori's speaker firmware, on your network.

Open the **Playing on** sheet, and nori looks for speakers while the sheet is open. Choose a speaker, and
the music plays there.

- nori sends the speaker up to a minute of music at a time, then sleeps until the speaker asks for more.
  The phone's Wi-Fi and CPU rest in between, and a pause or a seek is still heard at once.
- The speaker's buttons play, pause and skip. On Android, the phone's volume keys set its volume.
- The speaker must be on the same network as nori. A network that blocks devices from finding each other
  hides it.
- nori fetches each song as it always does and passes it to the speaker. Nothing goes to the internet
  for the speaker.
