# Orbit

Orbit makes the music circle your head, at the speed you set. It is best on headphones.

As the song passes each ear, that ear hears it louder and a little earlier. While the song is behind you,
it sounds duller.

## Settings

- **Speed** is how many turns the music makes each second, from 0.02 to 1. It starts at 0.12.
- **Width** is how far the music swings to each side, from 0 to 1. It starts at 0.8. At 1, you hear the
  song as one source that goes round you. Lower values keep more of the song's own stereo.

## Good to know

With Orbit on, nori cannot use offload. The CPU decodes the music, which uses more battery.
