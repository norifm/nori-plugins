# Orbit

Orbit makes the music circle your head, at the speed you set. It is best on headphones.

As the song passes each ear, that ear hears it louder and a little earlier. While the song is behind you,
it sounds duller. At a **Width** of 1, you hear the song as one source that goes round you. Lower values
keep more of the song's own stereo.

With Orbit on, nori cannot use offload. The CPU decodes the music, which uses more battery.
