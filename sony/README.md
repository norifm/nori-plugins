# Sony

Battery, noise control and settings for Sony headphones, in nori's player.

Tested on the WH-1000XM6. Other headphones that speak Sony's newer protocol (the WH-1000XM5, the
WF-1000XM4 and later, LinkBuds) show the settings they report having; unexpected things may happen on them.

The headphones' equalizer stays off, so it doesn't stack with nori's.

Close Sony's Sound Connect app while you use this. The headphones talk to one app at a time.

The protocol comes from [SonyHeadphonesClient](https://github.com/mos9527/SonyHeadphonesClient) by mos9527
and its contributors (MIT) and from [Gadgetbridge](https://codeberg.org/Freeyourgadget/Gadgetbridge)
(AGPL-3.0-or-later).
