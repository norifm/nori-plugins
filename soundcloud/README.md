# SoundCloud

SoundCloud adds SoundCloud profiles to nori, so you can search SoundCloud and play from it. Without
signing in, you see SoundCloud's trending songs and Discover shelves on Home. Signed in, you see your own
likes, playlists and stream.

To add one, turn the plugin on. Then, in **Settings › Profiles**, tap **Add profile** and choose
SoundCloud.

## Sign in

- **Sign In with a Code** gives you a code. Open its page in a browser that has your SoundCloud account,
  check the code and allow nori.
- **Sign in to SoundCloud** opens SoundCloud's own sign-in page in nori.
- **Paste cookies instead** takes the cookies of a signed-in SoundCloud session.

Signed in, Favorites holds your likes, and Artists holds those you follow. A favorite song or album is a
like on SoundCloud, and a favorite artist is a follow. Playlists you make in nori are private on
SoundCloud.

## What it sends

nori sends your searches and what you open and play to SoundCloud. Signed in, every request carries your
session, which nori keeps on this device. nori also reads SoundCloud's site and its scripts, for the key
the site uses.

## Good to know

- This is best effort: SoundCloud can stop it working at any time.
- nori leaves out songs that only subscribers hear in full, songs SoundCloud serves only encrypted, and
  albums with nothing else. Many labels' albums are like that.
- You cannot download SoundCloud songs.
- Signing in through a plugin may be against SoundCloud's terms. Music still plays without signing in.
