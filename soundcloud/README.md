# SoundCloud

SoundCloud adds SoundCloud profiles to nori, so you can search SoundCloud and play from it. Sign in to
see your own likes, playlists and stream.

To add one, install the plugin and turn it on. Then, in **Settings › Profiles**, tap **Add profile** and
choose SoundCloud. Without signing in, you can search, play and see SoundCloud's trending songs and
Discover shelves on Home.

## Sign in

- **Sign In with a Code** gives you a code. Open its page in a browser that has your SoundCloud account,
  check the code and allow nori.
- **Sign in to SoundCloud** opens SoundCloud's own sign-in page in nori.
- **Paste cookies instead** takes the cookies of a signed-in SoundCloud session.

Signed in, your library is your SoundCloud library. Favorites holds your likes. Albums and Playlists hold
those you made and those you liked. Artists holds those you follow. Home adds your recent plays, your
stream, your playlists and your likes.

A favorite song or album is a like on SoundCloud, and a favorite artist is a follow. Playlists you make
in nori are private on SoundCloud.

## What it sends

nori sends your searches and what you open and play to SoundCloud. Signed in, every request carries your
session, which nori keeps on this device. nori also reads SoundCloud's site and its scripts. They hold
the key that the site uses, and nori uses it too.

## Good to know

- This is best effort: SoundCloud can stop it working at any time.
- nori leaves out songs that only subscribers hear in full and songs SoundCloud serves only encrypted. It
  leaves out albums with nothing else, too. Many labels' albums are like that.
- You cannot download SoundCloud songs. nori fetches only the next song ahead.
- Signing in through a plugin may be against SoundCloud's terms. Music still plays without signing in.
